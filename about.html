About Infinity Holdings

Who We Are

Infinity Holdings is a diversified holding company whose purpose is to build, own, develop, invest in, and oversee businesses across multiple strategic industries.

Who We Are

A diversified holding company

Infinity Holdings provides strategic direction, governance, investment, capital allocation, operational oversight and long-term growth support to its subsidiaries and portfolio companies.

We are positioned as the parent organization — while every company under the Infinity Holdings umbrella maintains its own identity, brand and operations. It is not a single company offering unrelated services; it is a group of businesses operating under one strategic vision.

Our Vision

To build a diversified group of businesses that creates lasting value through innovation, responsible growth and strategic excellence.

Our Mission

To identify opportunities, build strong businesses, empower our companies and create sustainable value across industries and communities.
