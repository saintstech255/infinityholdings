Who We Are

One Group. Multiple Industries.
One Vision.

Infinity Holdings is built on a simple belief: opportunity exists everywhere, but meaningful growth requires vision, discipline and innovation. We bring together businesses across multiple industries and provide the strategic foundation they need to grow, adapt and create lasting value.

Innovation is not limited to one industry. It is the way we approach every industry — from the way Orbit Media tells a story to the way Nova Energy plans a power line.
